How to Embed in Notion

  1. Save or Download the File: Right-click anywhere on this page and select "Save Page As..." or click the save/download option to download this HTML file (notion_dashboard.html) to your device.
  2. Option A - Upload directly to Notion:

    In your Notion page, type /embed, select "Upload", and upload this downloaded notion_dashboard.html. It will run locally inside your Notion workspace, retaining all state!

  3. Option B - Host & Embed URL:

    Copy the Development/Production web URL of your LifeOS deployment, append /notion_dashboard.html to the end, paste it into Notion, and choose "Create Embed".

  4. Copy Markdown Data:

    Use the "Copy Notion Markdown" button at the top to export your daily status, sleep history, hydration logs, and mission metrics in clean markdown tables instantly copyable to Notion.
